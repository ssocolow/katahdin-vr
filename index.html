Fly Katahdin

A WebXR flight over Baxter Peak, rendered live from Google's Photorealistic 3D Tiles. Works flat on a desktop, or in full immersion on a Quest.

Needs a key with the Map Tiles API enabled — it stays on this page and is sent only to tile.googleapis.com. Don't have one? Follow Google's guide: create a Google Cloud project, enable the Map Tiles API, then copy your key from APIs & Services → Credentials.

Quest: open this page in the Quest browser and tap Enter VR. Move like Google Earth VR: hold trigger or grip and drag the world; fling and release to glide. Flick the right stick to snap-turn.
Desktop: the full control reference appears in the corner once you're flying.
